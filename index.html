From vision to reality

A home before it was ever a venue.

Peace on the Pond began with Crystal & Kevin Epps and a property with no architect and no fixed plan, just a shared decision to build something worth the faith it took. For more than two years they worked through the uncertainty of a full rebuild, at times apart, at times exhausted, but always returning to why they started.

They kept the bones of the original home out of respect for the land's history, so the place would be honored rather than erased. That care is still the standard the property is run by: a space meant to hold people gently, the way it held the Epps family through building it.

Crystal & Kevin Epps, Founders

Why this is hard

You came to hold space for your guests. Let the space hold you, too.

Between sessions, you're the one fielding where the towels are, what time lunch starts, and whether the chairs can move.

You're coordinating setup, catering, and last-minute changes instead of staying present with your group.

You want your guests to feel like they've stepped away from everything, which is hard when you're still holding the logistics.

Every venue question that lands on you is attention that isn't going to the people you came to serve.

Before you ask

Questions we get all the time.

What does it actually cost?+

Overnight retreats start at $3,223 per night, and day experiences start at $1,888 per day. Your exact quote depends on group size, nights or days, and any add-ons, and we build it with you on your consultation call.

How many guests can I bring?+

Up to 20 guests comfortably, with access to the lake, labyrinth, greenhouse, spa, and grounds.

Can I bring my own facilitators?+

Yes. You're welcome to bring your own team, or draw on our local wellness practitioners for yoga, massage, sound healing, and reiki.

What about food for my group?+

You can bring your own chef, or we can point you toward catering recommendations. Let us know what you need on your booking request.

How far is it from the airport?+

About 35 minutes from Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport.

How do I secure my date?+

An initial deposit holds your date once your details and quote are confirmed. Flexible payment plans are available, with final payment typically due 21 days before your event.
