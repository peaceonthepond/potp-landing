From vision to reality

A home before it was ever a venue.

Peace on the Pond began with Crystal and Kevin Epps and a shared vision to create something meaningful on ten acres of Georgia land.

What began as a deeply personal restoration became something larger: a place intentionally shaped for stillness, connection, reflection, and renewal.

Over more than two years, the property was thoughtfully reimagined while preserving the character of the original home and honoring the natural beauty surrounding it. Every space was created with intention: places to gather, places to breathe, places to reconnect, and places to simply be.

Today, that same intention guides every experience at Peace on the Pond. Before it became a destination for others, it became a sanctuary for us. Now, we have the privilege of opening the gates for you.

Good to know

Questions we get all the time.

What does it actually cost?+

Day retreat experiences begin at $1,888. Overnight retreats are custom quoted based on your group size, length of stay, selected experiences, culinary needs, and level of support. After learning about your vision, we'll create a proposal specifically for your retreat.

How many guests can I bring?+

Peace on the Pond can accommodate approximately 25–30 guests for day retreats and 10–12 guests for overnight retreats. We'll confirm the best setup for your group during your consultation.

Can I bring my own facilitators?+

Absolutely. Your retreat remains your own. Bring the practitioners and facilitators you trust, or explore our curated network of wellness and experiential professionals when additional support would serve your vision.

What about food for my group?+

Culinary support can be tailored to your retreat. Bring your preferred chef, or allow us to help coordinate catering and culinary options aligned with your group's needs.

How far is it from the airport?+

Peace on the Pond is located in Douglasville, Georgia, within convenient reach of Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport. Travel time varies based on traffic and time of day.

How do I secure my dates?+

Once your retreat details and proposal are confirmed, an initial deposit reserves your dates. Your agreement will outline the payment schedule and final balance due date.
