Show this calendar in Outlook

Subscribe once and your retreats, calls, and tours appear in Outlook automatically. Outlook refreshes it every few hours.

  1. Click Copy calendar link.
  2. In Outlook.com, open Calendar, then Add calendar, then Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the link, name it "Peace on the Pond," and click Import.

Keep this link private. If it's ever shared by mistake, make a new link and subscribe again.
