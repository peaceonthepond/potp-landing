The offsite problem

Most offsites feel a lot like the office.

The same windowless conference room, the same screens, the same patterns, just a different address.

Laptops stay open, notifications keep coming, and attention splits between the room and the inbox.

The agenda is packed wall to wall, leaving no room for the thinking and conversations you came for.

Your team heads home with a page of notes, but nothing has really shifted in how they work together.

Why Peace on the Pond

Room to focus. Room to breathe.

Peace on the Pond gives your team a private setting that moves easily between working sessions and real restoration. Strategy in the greenhouse, a walk on the trails between sessions, lunch on the terrace, and a fireside close by the water.

  • Only your teamThe whole estate is reserved for your group. No other meetings, no hotel lobby, no distractions.
  • Spaces for working and gatheringThe greenhouse, covered pavilion, and Serenity Terrace for sessions and breakouts, indoors and out.
  • Wellness built into the daySound and breathwork, the labyrinth, walking trails, infrared sauna, and jacuzzi to reset between sessions.
  • Facilitation when you want itVision casting, team values alignment, and leadership reflection, shaped around your goals. Or bring your own facilitator.
  • One point of contactOne person coordinating setup, timing, and any added services as your offsite comes together, with an on-site host available for the day.

Good to know

Questions teams ask us.

What does a corporate offsite cost?+

Corporate days start at $3,333 for exclusive use of the estate, with breakfast and light bites, for up to 50 attendees. Added services such as catering, an on-site host, or a facilitated workshop are priced separately, so you only pay for what you choose. Overnight Executive Retreats are custom quoted based on your group size, length of stay, menu, and sessions. Your proposal shows every line before anything is booked.

How many people can we bring?+

Day packages welcome up to 50 attendees, with no per-person venue fee; catering is priced per person. Overnight retreats host up to 10 guests in our five Gemstone Suites. We'll confirm the best setup for your group on our call.

Can we bring our own facilitator or speaker?+

Yes. Bring the facilitators and speakers you trust, add one of our facilitated workshops, or combine both. The agenda is yours.

What about food and dietary needs?+

Breakfast and light bites are included. Catered lunch and dinner can be added and are planned around your team's dietary needs and allergies. Private chef and hibachi dinners are available too.

Can we pay by invoice?+

Yes. You can pay online, or ask for an invoice for your accounts payable team. Let us know about any vendor setup or purchase order number you need, and we'll work through it with you.

How far is it from Atlanta?+

Peace on the Pond is in Douglasville, Georgia, west of Atlanta and within convenient reach of Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport. Travel time varies with traffic and time of day.
